# Wattle & Lash

### Lash, brow and eye beauty, shipped Australia-wide.

</div>

---

Wattle & Lash is an online beauty store that brings everything for lashes, brows and eyes into one place. Whether it's a first pair of strip lashes or a salon restock, shoppers can find, compare and order what they need from any device.

## The range

**225 products across 15 categories**, with vegan options throughout the store.

| Lashes | Lash care | Brows, eyes and more |
|---|---|---|
| Strip lashes | Serums | Mascaras |
| Individual clusters | Adhesives and glues | Brow products |
| Extension kits | Primers and bonders | Eye creams and under-eye care |
| Lift and perm kits | Removers and cleansers | Gift sets and bundles |
| Curlers and tools | Storage and accessories | Beauty supplements |

## The shopping experience

- **Easy to browse:** search, sort, and filter by category, price, vegan or in-stock products
- **Clear pricing:** every price is in Australian dollars and includes GST
- **Simple shipping:** $9.95 standard shipping Australia-wide, free on orders over $100
- **Flexible checkout:** order as a guest or with an account, and use discount codes
- **Order tracking:** check an order's status at any time
- **Customer accounts:** see past orders and save delivery addresses
- **Made for every screen:** a clean layout on desktop, with an app-style navigation bar on phones and tablets

## Behind the scenes

A dedicated admin portal keeps the store running day to day:

- **Dashboard** with an overview of the store
- **Orders** with status updates, tracking numbers and CSV export
- **Products and categories** with photo uploads
- **Discount codes** for promotions
- **Customers, messages and newsletter subscribers** in one place
- **Admin accounts** with role-based access

## Built with

Next.js · React · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS · MongoDB · Cloudinary
