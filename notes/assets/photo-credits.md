## Product stand-ins
`public/images/products/<sku>.webp` are crops of the Pexels photos listed per category in `POOLS` in `scripts/product-images.py`. Several crops come from each photo, so every product's file is different, but photos do recur within a category. They illustrate the category, not the exact product.
